EXPERIMENT 2: MOMENT OF INERTIA OF A DISC

Aim      : To determine the moment of inertia of a uniform disc about its
           central axis using a torsional pendulum.

Apparatus : Torsional pendulum, disc, vernier calipers, stopwatch, timer.

Procedure  :
 1. Measure the radius and thickness of the disc using vernier calipers.
 2. Record the equilibrium position of the inner and outer cylinders.
 3. Displace the disc by a small angle and release it.
 4. Time 20 oscillations and compute the mean period T.
 5. Compute I = (k T^2) / (4 pi^2).

Observations to be recorded in a table:
 Trial | Time for 20 oscillations (s) | Period T (s) | 4 pi^2 I / (k T^2)

Result: Moment of inertia of the disc in kg m^2, with units.
